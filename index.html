About Us

The Manibaug Paralaya Youth Press Corps (MPYPC) is a youth-led media organization committed to nonpartisan, ethical journalism under the Philippine Press Institute’s standards. Recognized by Barangay Manibaug Paralaya, we operate independently to promote transparency and civic engagement.

Our Structure

  • Press Adviser: Founder and guardian of MPYPC’s mission.
  • Editor-in-Chief (EIC): Oversees content and operations.
  • Department Heads: Manage news, opinion, sports, features, investigative, photo, and multimedia beats.
  • Editorial Board: Advises on policy and governance.
